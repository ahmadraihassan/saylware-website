# Saylware outreach kit (internal)

This folder is **not part of the website**. It is a private playbook so you can
get conversations with real operators using your LinkedIn and
`awaisu@saylware.com` — without looking like spam, and without hurting
`saylware.com`.

I cannot log into LinkedIn or send mail for you. What this kit gives you is
the targeting, the research habit, and **copy you can send today**.

## How this is meant to work

1. **LinkedIn is the front door.** Connection notes and short DMs do not
   touch your domain reputation.
2. **Email is the second door**, used one-to-one, only after a LinkedIn
   reply *or* when the company publishes a business contact on their own
   site. Never from a purchased list. Never in BCC. Never from a sequencer.
3. **Every note is written for that business.** If you cannot name one
   specific thing about them, do not send yet. Spend eight minutes on the
   research checklist instead.

## Do this today (about 45 minutes)

1. Read `rules.md` once. Those limits protect the domain.
2. Paste the profile copy in `assets/linkedin-profile.md`.
3. Paste the signature in `assets/email-signature.html` into Gmail / Outlook.
4. Confirm SPF, DKIM, and DMARC (`email-and-domain.md`). If those are not
   in place, **do not send cold email yet**. LinkedIn only.
5. Open `leads/batch-01.md`. Send the first **five LinkedIn connection
   notes**. Stop there for the day.
6. Log them in `tracker.csv`.

Tomorrow: five more connections from the same batch, plus one follow-up
to anyone who accepted yesterday.

## What is in here

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `rules.md` | Hard limits. Domain, legal, anti-phishing tone. |

| `email-and-domain.md` | When to email, DNS, warming, what never to send. |

| `assets/` | Profile copy and email signature. |

## Voice (keep this)

Calm. Specific. Short. Plain language. One ask. No urgency theater, no
fake familiarity, no “just bumping this,” no attachments on first touch.

Saylware’s site already sounds like this. Outreach should sound like the
same person.
