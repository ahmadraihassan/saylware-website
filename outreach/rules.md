# Rules that keep this human (and keep the domain healthy)

These are not style tips. Breaking them is how inboxes start treating
`@saylware.com` as junk, and how a note starts to feel like a scam.

## Hard no

- No purchased, scraped, or “lead list” emails.
- No mail merge, sequencer, or “500 emails a day” tool on `saylware.com`.
- No BCC blasts. No CC of strangers. One person, one thread.
- No fake “following up on our call” if you never spoke.
- No “your account / invoice / password / shipment” language. Ever.
- No shortened links (`bit.ly`, `tinyurl`). No tracking pixels you would
  be embarrassed to explain.
- No attachments or calendars on the first email. One optional link:
  `https://saylware.com`.
- No scraping LinkedIn. Search, read, and type like a person.
- No messaging people who asked you to stop. Mark `do_not_contact` in the
  tracker and leave them alone.

## Volume (this is what protects reputation)

| Channel | Daily cap | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn connection notes | 8–12 | LinkedIn throttles new accounts. Stay boring. |
| LinkedIn DMs (after they accept) | 5–8 | Only people who already said yes to connecting. |
| Cold email from `@saylware.com` | **0 until DNS is set**, then 3–5 | New domains die from volume, not from silence. |
| Follow-ups | 1 LinkedIn + 1 email, then stop | A third bump is where it becomes spam. |

If a day is busy, send fewer. Never “catch up” with 40 notes on Sunday.

## Identity (this is what stops the phishing feel)

Every email must make it obvious:

- Who you are (name + Saylware)
- What Saylware is (one clause, not a brochure)
- Why you wrote *them* (one fact from their business)
- How to say no (“If this is a miss, say so and I will close the loop.”)

Send from `awaisu@saylware.com`. Display name: your real name, e.g.
`Awais · Saylware`. Never `Security Desk`, `Accounts`, or `Support Team`.

## Legal baseline (US / CAN-SPAM + common sense)

- Only email addresses the company published, or people who wrote you first.
- Include a real signature: name, company, `saylware.com`, phone
  `+1 915 340 2356`, and a postal address once you have one on file.
- Honor opt-outs the same day.
- Do not email personal Gmail/Hotmail for a company pitch unless that
  person listed it as their work contact.

UK, UAE, Saudi, Canada, and Pakistan contacts: **LinkedIn first**. Email
only after they reply or publish a business address.

## What “personalized” actually means

A note is personalized when it would be wrong if you swapped the company
name. “Loved your growth journey” is not personal. “You still pick up
after the install instead of hiding behind fine print” is.

If you used ChatGPT to draft and the draft could fit any HVAC shop,
rewrite it.
