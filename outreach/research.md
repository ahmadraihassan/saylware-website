# Eight-minute research (for every new company)

Do this before you write. The batch-01 briefs already did it. Use this
when you add the next twelve.

## 1. Confirm they are a fit (2 min)

- Website: service company, SaaS, or ops-heavy SMB. Not a giant.
- LinkedIn company page: size, location, recent posts.
- Skip if they clearly have an in-house SOC or a 200-seat contact center.

## 2. Steal one true detail (4 min)

Pick **one**:

- A sentence from their homepage that only they would write
- An owner named on BBB / About / LinkedIn
- A hiring post (CS, dispatcher, office, technician)
- Hours: evenings, Saturday, “24/7”, “emergency”
- Reviews that praise (or complain about) the phone / follow-up
- A product they just launched
- Texas presence → SB 2610 is allowed as a *secondary* line, not the hook

Write that detail in the tracker `hook` column in your own words.

## 3. Pick the door (1 min)

- Care, or security. Not both.
- One person to write. Owner first.

## 4. Write, then cut (1 min)

Connection note under ~280 characters. DM under 90 words. Email under
120 words. Read it once out loud.

## Where to find the next batch

Stay in public, boring places:

- Google Maps: `pest control El Paso`, `auto repair El Paso`,
  `HVAC El Paso`, `security systems El Paso`, then the same for Las
  Cruces, Midland, Albuquerque.
- BBB profiles (owners are often named).
- LinkedIn search: `Owner` + `El Paso` + `pest` / `automotive` / `HVAC`.
- Their own careers page, not scraped emails.
- Warm intros: anyone you already know in El Paso, UAE, or Pakistan who
  can introduce you. An intro beats a perfect cold note.

Cap a new batch at 12. Finish sending batch-01 before you research
batch-02.
